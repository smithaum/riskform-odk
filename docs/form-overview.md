# 📋 Form Overview

## 🛡️ Criminal Risk Assessment Request

This project converts the provided **Criminal Risk Assessment Request** document into an **ODK XLSForm**.

The original form contains information required for conducting a Criminal Risk Assessment, including consent information, personal identifying information, identification details, and information about the agency submitting the request.

---

## 🎯 Main Sections

### ✍️ 1. Consent Information

This section captures:

- 📅 Date
- ✍️ Signature of the person being assessed
- 👤 Unconsented witness information

---

### 👤 2. Personal Information

This section captures:

- 🧑 First name
- 🧑 Second name
- 🧑 Last name
- 🎂 Date of birth
- ⚧️ Gender
- 📝 Other last names used
- 📝 Other first names used / also goes by
- 🏠 Current address
- 📞 Current phone numbers
- 🌍 City/Province or Country of Birth

---

### 🪪 3. Identification Information

The form includes identification options such as:

- 📄 Birth Certificate
- 💳 Social Insurance Card
- 🏥 Manitoba Health Card
- 🪪 Treaty Card
- 📑 Other identification
- 🚗 Manitoba Driver's License with Photo

---

### 📝 4. Risk Assessment Request Information

The second page captures:

- 👤 Name of person being assessed
- 🏢 Name of agency submitting the request
- ⚠️ Reason for risk assessment
- 👨‍💼 Assigned worker
- 📅 Date of last criminal risk assessment
- 👤 Submitting designate
- 📞 Designate phone number
- 📧 Designate email
- 📠 Designate fax
- 📅 Request date

---

## 🔴 Required Fields

The original document states that sections marked with an asterisk (`*`) are required.

These requirements are reflected in the XLSForm where applicable.

---

## 💡 Purpose of the Digital Form

The purpose of the XLSForm is to provide a structured digital representation of the original **Criminal Risk Assessment Request** form while maintaining its fields, choices, and required information.
